QNet Wallet is a non-custodial wallet for the QNet network. Keys are created on your device and never leave it. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

WALLET
• Create a wallet or import one from a BIP39 seed phrase
• Send and receive QNC and QNet tokens; show a QR code of your address
• Transaction history that outlives node pruning — every entry opens in the block explorer
• Filter history by asset
• Fingerprint, face or password unlock

LIGHT NODE (OPTIONAL)
The app can run a QNet light node. A few times per four-hour epoch the network sends your phone a status request; the app signs it and answers from the background, without being opened. Between requests the app only wakes now and then to send one signed attestation per epoch; nothing is computed beyond signing. Each epoch's answers are recorded on chain, and that epoch's emission is shared among the nodes that answered. Rewards are claimed from the app as an ordinary on-chain transaction.

This version does not activate new nodes. A wallet that already holds a node activation on chain recovers its activation code in the app and runs its light node from there.

POST-QUANTUM CRYPTOGRAPHY
Transactions are signed on the device with ML-DSA-65 (NIST FIPS 204). The QNet network's transport uses ML-KEM-768 hybrid key exchange.

OPEN SOURCE
The app, the browser extension and the explorer are open source under Apache-2.0; the node is source-available under the Business Source License 1.1. All of it is one public repository, so anyone can read exactly what the app does and build it.

LANGUAGES
English, with partial translations into Chinese (Simplified), Russian, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Arabic and Italian.

GOOD TO KNOW
• QNet is an experimental network at the testnet stage. Tokens may have no monetary value; rewards are decided by the network protocol and are not guaranteed.
• Your seed phrase is the only backup. Nobody can recover it for you.
• A light node needs the app to stay installed and allowed to run in the background: https://aiqnet.io/support
