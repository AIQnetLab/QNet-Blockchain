• A new design of the whole app: rounded cards, a tab bar with icons, round token icons and new fonts
• Tokens work in a new way: Send opens a list of your tokens with a search, and each token has its own screen
• The Node tab shows your node steadily, light or super, as the network names it
• Move to wallet takes the whole node balance in one press and shows what reached the wallet
• After a wallet is deleted the welcome screen opens; after a reset, the recovery phrase screen opens
